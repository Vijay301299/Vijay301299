03 · Privacy-First Architecture
AI-Powered Tool · GST Compliance
GST & Bank Reconciliation Tool

A fully browser-based reconciliation tool built for auditors and GST practitioners. Combines AI Vision/OCR, pdf.js, and SheetJS to match GSTR-2B ITC entries against bank statements — entirely on the client's device. No data ever leaves the browser. Built with a strict privacy-first architecture: sessionStorage only, CSP-enforced, zero cookies.

Core Capabilities
◈
GSTR-2B ITC Matching
Upload GSTR-2B PDF and bank statement. AI extracts and matches ITC entries against bank debits — flags mismatches, missing credits, and excess claims automatically.
◈
AI Vision / OCR Processing
Uses Claude Vision to read scanned PDFs and bank statements. No pre-formatting required — works with real-world messy documents straight from tally exports or bank portals.
◈
Zero-Storage Privacy Design
All data lives in sessionStorage and is wiped on tab close. No localStorage, no cookies, no server calls for client data. CSP headers enforced inline — designed for CA client confidentiality compliance.
